# Investment Evidence Audit

Review an investment workpaper for evidence discipline before circulation.

[中文镜像 / Chinese mirror](zh-CN/README.md)

## When to Use

Use this skill as the evidence boundary gate before sharing quick-look analysis, risk radar, DD question maps, or investment workpapers.

## What It Produces

- claim-support and classification findings
- authority, independence, date/currentness, and conflict checks
- inference, Unknown, stale-evidence, and state-overreach findings
- overclaim and generated-analysis-as-evidence flags
- bounded rewrites, required evidence, Human Review, and circulation boundary

## Evidence Discipline

This skill follows the repository's [Shared Evidence Semantics](../../EVIDENCE_SEMANTICS.md). It audits a draft; it does not discover evidence, triage the initial source package, or approve circulation.

## AI InvestOS Module Mapping

Evidence boundary audit / quality gate
