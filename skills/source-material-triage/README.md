# Source Material Triage

Identify what each project material can and cannot support before analysis begins.

[中文镜像 / Chinese mirror](zh-CN/README.md)

## When to Use

Use this skill before any quick-look analysis or investment workpaper drafting, especially when the material package is uneven, source-limited, or mixed across company claims, interview notes, and internal analyst notes.

## What It Produces

- material inventory
- source type, qualitative authority, date/currentness, and conflict status
- material maturity assessment
- usable evidence boundary
- minimum relevant context and missing evidence
- human review notes

## Evidence Discipline

This skill follows the repository's [Shared Evidence Semantics](../../EVIDENCE_SEMANTICS.md). It preserves company claims, independent evidence, inference, Unknowns, conflicts, and temporal boundaries without forming an investment judgment or launching full external research.
