# Primary-Market Quick-Look

Create a first-pass investor-style view of a company without overstating the evidence.

[中文镜像 / Chinese mirror](zh-CN/README.md)

## When to Use

Use this skill when an investor, founder, analyst, or strategy team needs to understand a company through an investment research lens from limited but usable materials.

## What It Produces

- one-paragraph quick look
- project essence
- investment logic
- highlight analysis
- counter-hypotheses
- risk summary
- evidence gaps
- next verification priorities

## Evidence Discipline

This skill must separate facts, company claims, interview notes, financial snapshots, analytical inferences, missing evidence, and items that need human review.

## AI InvestOS Module Mapping

Project essence / investment logic / highlights / counter-hypotheses
